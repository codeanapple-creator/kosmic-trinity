On kosmictrinity.in, mobile screens experience severe visual flickering during 
scrolling — this is separate from the earlier menu/navigation fixes (commits 
22bb0bb, 07bdd07, 9022d90) and should not undo any of them. Diagnose the mobile 
rendering issue and fix it.

Specifically, on screens below 768px:

1. Disable or simplify all scroll-triggered opacity/transform reveal animations — 
   e.g. the fade-in cards like "Ancient Wisdom, Present Clarity" and the "Sacred 
   Offerings" (Dharma/Artha/Kaam) cards that animate in as you scroll. On mobile, 
   these should render at opacity:1 / final position by default, not animate in 
   per-scroll.
2. Disable or simplify any backdrop-filter/blur effects (e.g. on the chat widget 
   panel or menu overlay), animated gradients, the constellation/particle background 
   layer behind the hero circle, and any other continuously-animated background 
   (e.g. the pulsing glow behind the Ganesha/Bodhi chat icon) on mobile — these are 
   likely GPU compositing every scroll frame and causing jank/flicker on Android in 
   particular.
3. Audit for elements that repeatedly mount/unmount or re-trigger their animation 
   as they enter/exit the viewport during scroll (e.g. an IntersectionObserver-based 
   reveal that re-fires every time a section crosses the threshold in either 
   direction, instead of firing once). Fix so animations trigger once and stay 
   settled, or are skipped entirely on mobile.
4. Check for sticky/fixed positioned elements (the navbar, the chat toggle button) 
   that might be causing repaint/reflow thrashing on scroll on Android specifically.
5. Keep the visual design intact on desktop — this should be a mobile-only 
   (< 768px) simplification, not a redesign, and must not regress the menu-flicker, 
   chat-widget-overlap, or navigation-flash fixes already deployed.

After the fix, verify smooth scrolling on an actual Android device or emulator 
(not just desktop responsive mode) — scroll the full homepage top to bottom and 
back, and confirm no flicker, no repeated fade-ins, and no visible jank.
